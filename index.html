NPCI Unified Payments Interface Protocol Architecture

How QR codes, Intent URIs, invoices, and payment audio broadcast across the UPI ecosystem.

URI Structure & Parameter Keys

Every UPI transaction uses upi://pay? followed by RFC-compliant query parameters. Key parameters include: pa (Payee VPA), pn (Payee Name), am (Amount in INR), cu (Currency code, must be INR), tr (Transaction Reference ID), tn (Note/Memo), mc (Merchant Category Code), mam (Minimum Amount Allowed), and sign (Base64 PKI Signature).

Zero Payment Gateway Commissions

By routing directly through the NPCI peer-to-peer (P2P) or peer-to-merchant (P2M) deep link protocol, funds travel directly from payer bank account to payee bank account without middleman gateways deducting 2% to 3% platform fees.
