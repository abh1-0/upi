NPCI Unified Payments Interface Protocol Anatomy

How QR codes and Intent URIs decode across PSP apps.

URI Structure

Every UPI checkout begins with the custom scheme upi://pay followed by query parameters. Mobile operating systems (Android Intent Filters & iOS Universal Links) parse this scheme and pass the dictionary of key-value pairs to verified payment apps (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM).

Desktop vs Mobile Handshake

Because desktop browsers lack native UPI hardware token storage, attempting to launch upi:// directly will trigger an unhandled protocol error. The recommended merchant pattern is displaying the dynamic QR standee for smartphone camera scans.
