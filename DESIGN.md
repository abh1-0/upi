# UPI Studio - Design System & Tokens (Premier Design)

## Design Read
`Operate mode workbench for fintech engineers and Indian retail merchants with an Apple HIG + Liquid Glass tactile language leaning toward Linear-clean developer productivity.`

## The Three Dials
- **DESIGN_VARIANCE**: 6 (Structured workbench symmetry with focused focal standee)
- **MOTION_INTENSITY**: 5 (Crisp 150-250ms spring feedback, tactile button depressions, active scale-[0.98])
- **VISUAL_DENSITY**: 7 (Data-rich workbench layout with 8pt spatial rhythm)

## Spatial Foundation (Apple HIG 8pt Grid)
- Micro: 4px, 8px
- Component internal: 12px, 16px, 20px
- Section gap: 24px, 32px, 48px
- Minimum touch targets: >= 44px on mobile, >= 32px desktop.

## Color Palette & Materials
- **Canvas Dark**: `#070A12` (Deep Obsidian Void)
- **Card Surface**: `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.72)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px) saturate(180%)`
- **Liquid Glass Highlights**: `border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, specular top rim reflection.
- **Accents**:
  - Primary UPI Emerald: `#00D26A`
  - High-Speed Beam Blue: `#0084FF`
  - Deep Navy Accent: `#1E293B`
  - Light Canvas: `#F8FAFC`
  - Light Surface: `#FFFFFF`
